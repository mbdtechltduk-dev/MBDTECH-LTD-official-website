Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how MBDTECH LTD uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember basic preferences, improve performance, and understand how visitors interact with pages.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate our website, improve user experience, measure website performance, protect forms from misuse, understand marketing results, and support advertising campaign measurement.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for website navigation, page loading, forms, security, and basic functionality.
  • Performance cookies: Help us understand page visits, loading issues, and general website performance.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand which pages are visited and how users interact with our website.
  • Advertising cookies: May help measure Google Ads performance and actions taken after users click an advertisement.
  • Preference cookies: May remember basic choices such as cookie consent or interface preferences.
  • Security cookies: May help protect forms, prevent spam, and maintain website reliability.

4. Google Ads, Google Tag and Measurement

Our website may use Google tag, Google Ads conversion tracking, remarketing tags, or similar measurement tools. These technologies may use cookies or device identifiers to help measure ad performance, understand conversions, and improve campaign relevance.

5. Third-Party Cookies and External Links

Our website may link to or interact with third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Play, Apple App Store, payment providers, analytics services, advertising platforms, hosting providers, and embedded tools. These third parties may use their own cookies and privacy practices.

6. Cookie Consent

When you visit our website, you may see a cookie notice. By clicking “Accept”, you agree that we may use cookies and similar technologies as described in this policy. You can also manage or block cookies through your browser settings.

7. Managing or Blocking Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to delete cookies, block cookies, or receive a warning before cookies are stored. If you block cookies, some website features may not work correctly.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, analytics tools, advertising tools, or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page.
